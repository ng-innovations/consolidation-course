Financial Report Consolidation

A study curriculum for developers and analysts working on the consolidation engine.

This course teaches financial consolidation from first principles. It assumes basic bookkeeping familiarity but no prior consolidation experience. The structure mirrors the way our application is built, so concepts you learn here map directly to code you will write.

Click any highlighted term in a lesson to open its glossary entry in the right panel. Every accounting term, abbreviation, and concept that a non-accountant might not know has an entry.
