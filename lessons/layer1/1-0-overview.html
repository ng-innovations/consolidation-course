Layer 1 — Overview

Purpose & Concepts

Layer summary

Layer 1 introduces what financial consolidation is, why companies do it, who's involved, how the work flows end-to-end, and what feeds in and comes out. It's deliberately accounting-light — no journal entries, no FX rate types, no goodwill math — so the focus stays on framing the problem before the mechanics. Seven short lessons, about an hour to read.

What you should take away

By the end of Layer 1 you should be able to explain consolidation in plain language, name the steps in the workflow and why the order matters, distinguish parent / subsidiary / associate / joint venture, identify the four primary outputs and what's distinctive about each, describe the source data the engine consumes, and articulate why companies eventually outgrow Excel. You won't yet be able to do the math — that's Layer 2.
